Common Questions

How long does a project take?

A logo usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. App or website designs can take 3 to 6 weeks. I give you a clear date before we start.

How many changes can I ask for?

Each project includes rounds of changes. We agree on the number before work begins.

What files will I get?

You get files for print and for screens, such as PDF, PNG, SVG and the source files when needed.

How do I start?

Send me a message on the contact page. Tell me about your idea and I will reply with the next steps.
